## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-21

### Added

- Always show the Workflow ID picker for every diagram type, even with one workflow.
- Filter choices to Workflow processes and reject missing/non-Workflow IDs in the bridge.
- Show the selected workflow in the preview title and caption.
- Retain the separate cwl2puml class-template closing-marker workaround.
